# Beyond TV source rights transition

Keep the Watch experience working while reviewing sources item by item. The
review record is `data/source-rights-review.json`; it does not alter playback.

## Verification standard

Mark an exact video `verified_for_beyond_tv` only after recording:

1. The source file or provider URL and the identity and authority of the grantor.
2. A written license, applicable public-domain analysis, or another specific legal basis.
3. Coverage for public streaming in the web, iOS and Android apps, intended
   territories, advertising or membership use, duration, and any editing.
4. Separate music, performance, artwork, restoration, subtitle and other embedded
   rights where applicable.
5. Required attribution and a working way to display it outside the player.
6. The evidence link or stored agreement, reviewer, date and next review date.

An Internet Archive item, a playable link, an internal approval, and a provider's
embed button are source information. None alone completes this review.

## First pass

Review the highest-visibility commercial series first. Prefer a documented
license or a cleared replacement for each scheduled item. Review promising
Commons and public-domain items in parallel, including their credits and embedded
rights. Do not relabel a whole channel as verified because one item passes.

When an item passes, add it to the active rotation in a small batch and retain
the existing channel layout and controls. When evidence is missing, keep its
record as `rights_unverified`; do not imply clearance in public labels.

The initial ledger records two source pages with Creative Commons licenses and
one public-domain claim. They are candidates for the first clearance batch,
not completed Beyond TV clearances.
