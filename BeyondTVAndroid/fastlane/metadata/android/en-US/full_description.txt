Source Safety is built into Beyond TV. Open it when a video link raises a rights, security, or playback concern, and send the exact source for review.

Reports include the source link, the Beyond TV page, the video title, and your observation. They enter a private review queue so the Beyond TV team can investigate. A working link does not confirm that a video is licensed or in the public domain.

The live TV experience stays front and center: browse Beyond TV channels and the video catalog, watch available streams, and follow the live-style guide. Some videos come from external providers, whose availability may change.
