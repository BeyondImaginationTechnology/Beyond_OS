Pluto is smaller than Earth’s Moon. The claim you could drive around it in a day is a cartoon joke, not a scientific measurement.
Pluto has five known moons: Charon, Styx, Nix, Kerberos, and Hydra. Charon is biggest, and the pair orbit a shared point beyond Pluto.
Pluto is still a dwarf planet. That classification describes a world that orbits the Sun, is round, and has not cleared its orbital neighborhood.
Pluto takes about 248 Earth years to orbit the Sun. This diagram is playful artwork, not a scale model, and its labels are inaccurate.
Its heart-shaped region is Tombaugh Regio. The Sputnik Planitia plain is rich in nitrogen ice; the heart doesn’t prove a warm interior.
