Daily Breath scripture sources

The app keeps these English editions offline and identifies them as translations
or meanings rather than replacements for the original sacred texts.

- English Bible and English Torah/Tanakh: World English Bible (WEB), public
  domain. Source: https://ebible.org/details.php?id=engwebp
- English Quran meaning: The Meaning of the Glorious Koran, translated by
  Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall. Source: Project Gutenberg eBook 16955,
  https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/16955 (public domain in the United States).

Before enabling distribution in a new territory, confirm that the included
editions may be distributed there and retain any required attribution.
