# Scripture sources

The Android Daily Breath app bundles the source record shown to users in
`app/src/main/assets/SCRIPTURE_SOURCES.txt`.

- English Bible and English Torah/Tanakh: World English Bible (WEB), public
  domain. Source: <https://ebible.org/details.php?id=engwebp>.
- English Quran meaning: *The Meaning of the Glorious Koran*, translated by
  Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall. Source: [Project Gutenberg eBook 16955](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/16955), public domain in the United States.

Confirm territorial distribution rights before enabling a country and preserve
the attribution in the app and store materials.
