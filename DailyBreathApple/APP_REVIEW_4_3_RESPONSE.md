# App Review response — Guideline 4.3(a)

Thank you for reviewing The Daily Breath. We understand the concern about apps that share a binary, metadata, template, or concept with other submissions.

This build is an offline-first interfaith recovery and spiritual-wellness product with the following reviewable functionality:

- Three distinct faith journeys—Christian, Jewish, and Muslim—with tradition-specific daily readings, devotionals/reflections, prayers, recovery practices, academy paths, guides, and themes.
- A complete offline multilingual sacred-text library: English/French/Spanish Bible editions; Hebrew Torah/Tanakh by default with English/French translations; and Arabic Quran by default with a Pickthall English meaning.
- Tradition-native terminology and navigation. For example, the Jewish reader presents Bereshit, Shemot, and Tehillim instead of reusing Christian Bible labels, while the Quran reader uses surahs and ayahs.
- Native right-to-left Hebrew and Arabic reading, full-text search, chapter navigation, daily matching, widgets, journaling, breathing practices, recovery challenges, and optional encrypted iCloud user-data sync.
- Bundled, attributed, version-pinned scripture resources that work offline.

Review paths:

- Open **Scripture** in the tab bar. Use **Faith tradition** to switch among Bible, Torah, and Quran, then use **Language & edition** to inspect the available offline texts.
- Choose **Torah**, keep the default Hebrew edition, and open **תהילים · Tehillim** to verify the Jewish book name and right-to-left Hebrew reader. English and French translations are available from the edition picker.
- Choose **Quran** to verify the default Arabic right-to-left reader, surah/ayah terminology, and the optional Pickthall English meaning.
- Open **Today** and change the tradition picker to verify tradition-specific readings, reflections, prayers, themes, and recovery practices.
- Open **Academy** to review the separate Christian, Jewish, and Muslim Joining the Faith and Recovery journeys.

We respectfully request a new review of this materially differentiated build. If a particular binary, asset, metadata phrase, or other submitted app triggered the similarity determination, please identify it so we can address that specific concern.
